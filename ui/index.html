Money found, released and protected

A shopkeeper sends a supplier bill. StockMate extracts it, re-does the arithmetic and the GST, audits the prices against what this shop paid before, finds the cash sitting in slow or expiring stock, and proposes an order that fits the cash actually available this week.

House rule: code decides, the language layer only explains.

Money found

Overcharges, arithmetic slips and GST errors on supplier bills, each one traceable to the line it came from.

Money released

Capital locked in stock that is turning slowly or has stopped moving altogether, and can be freed.

Money protected

Value that will be lost at expiry unless the stock clears first, flagged while there is still time to act.

Chat

The conversational front door, as a shopkeeper would use it. Ask what to buy, what is trapped, and what was found on the bills.

Dashboard

Headline figures, every finding with its working shown, and the order plan inside a cash budget.
